# YouTube SEO: "What Actually Happens When Noah Checks Your PC?"

Format: YouTube Short (45s, 9:16). Primary search intent: *"why is my PC slow"*, *"slow PC fix"*, *"what does this PC app actually do"*.

## Title (pick one; front-load the search phrase, keep it ≤ 60 characters)

1. **Your PC Is Slow. Here's What Noah Actually Checks** ← recommended
2. What Actually Happens When Noah Checks Your PC?
3. Slow PC? What Noah Does Before It Changes Anything

Put `#Shorts` in the description, not the title. YouTube classifies vertical videos under 3 minutes as Shorts automatically.

#Shorts #SlowPC #PCTips #Windows #TechTips

Notes:
- The first ~100 characters show in search and under the Short, so the hook line and the problem keyword come first.
- Keep the link as a full `https://` URL so it's clickable on desktop. On Shorts the most reliable link is the pinned comment (below) or the **Related video** field.
- Limit hashtags to 3–5. More than that dilutes them, and YouTube ignores every hashtag on a video that has more than 60.
