# Noah for Mac: 5 YouTube Shorts scripts

Draft v1. Each video is 25–35 seconds, 9:16. Product facts come only from onnoah.app/mac.

## How every script is built to get views

- **0–1.5s hook:** a line that sounds like the viewer's own problem, big on-screen text, and the actual Mac in frame. No logo intro.
- **Say "Noah" out loud by 0:10.** YouTube's auto-captions are searchable, so saying it is SEO.
- **On-screen text repeats the search phrase** (e.g. "SYSTEM DATA"), so the video matches search even when muted.
- **One "Noah moment" per video:** Noah names the cause, shows one fix, you tap Go ahead. That's the shareable beat.
- **Loop ending:** the last line or image leads back into the first, which boosts replays.
- **Honest claims only:** free to start, shows the plan first, changes only what you approve, cleared files go to the Trash, every change is logged, and it says plainly when it's hardware. No speed guarantees, no "virus cleaning".

Notes in [brackets] are visuals or on-screen text. **VO** is the voice-over.

## SEO checklist for all 5

- **File name:** match the keyword before uploading, e.g. `mac-system-data-storage-full-noah.mp4`.
- **Title:** keyword first, under ~60 characters, `#shorts` at the end.
- **Description first line:** the exact search phrase. "Noah for Mac" plus `https://onnoah.app/mac/` in every description.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 in the description, the first 3 show above the title.
- **Say "Noah" in the voice-over** (auto-captions are indexed), and show "Noah for Mac" on screen at least once.
- **Upload your own captions file** (exact script) so spelling of "Noah" and the terms like "System Data" and "mds_stores" is right.
- **Pinned comment asks a question** that viewers answer from their own Mac. That drives comments, which drives reach.
- **Playlist:** "Fix Your Mac in 30 Seconds", one place for all 5 (helps watch time and search).
- **Post cadence:** one a day for 5 days, best first (suggested order: 1, 3, 5, 2, 4).
- **Same posts on TikTok:** reuse the title as the caption plus the hashtags. Instagram Reels the same.
