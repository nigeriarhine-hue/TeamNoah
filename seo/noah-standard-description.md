# Noah standard description (boilerplate)

Append this to the description of every Noah video / post, after the video-specific copy and the link.

## Canonical (Mac) version — as provided by the Noah team

```
Noah is an app that diagnoses and fixes real problems on your Mac. You describe the symptom in plain language ("my fan won't stop"), Noah finds what's actually wrong with your Mac — and shows you before it touches anything.

Your Mac is slow, or full, or something broke after an update. You tell Noah what's wrong in your own words. It investigates like a technician, names the actual cause, and proposes a one-sentence fix. Nothing runs until you approve. Then it's fixed, logged, and reversible. Also, the client is open source.
```
