# Storyboard — "Fifteen Minutes"

34.000s · 24fps · 816 frames · 21 clips · 2.39:1

`LIVE` = generated in Runway. `PLATE+UI` = Runway plate with the Noah interface screen-replaced
in After Effects. `GFX` = built entirely in After Effects.

## ACT I — THE INVISIBLE DRAIN
**00:00:00 – 00:10:20 (frames 0–260, 10.83s)**

A problem you cannot see, happening faster than it should.

**01 · LIVE · 3.25s · 00:00:00**
Wide, night home office. She sits at the walnut desk in the charcoal crewneck, face lit from
below by the screen, desk lamp warm on her left. Everything is quiet and ordinary. Slow dolly
push begins. *This shot has to earn the word "premium" — hold it, don't cut early.*

**02 · PLATE+UI · 1.79s · 00:03:06**
Extreme close-up on the top-right corner of the screen. The charging bolt is lit. **41%.** The
focus slips soft for four frames and returns on **38%** — two moments, not one impossible one. It
is losing charge while plugged in.

**03 · LIVE · 1.58s · 00:05:01**
Close-up, her face. She sees it. Not a grimace — the small, tired narrowing of someone who has
noticed this before and is out of theories.

**04 · LIVE · 1.58s · 00:06:15**
Macro, her hand. Fingers close on the USB-C connector and pull it from the port. She is running
a test. The compact charger sits in frame, obviously small.

**05 · PLATE+UI · 1.42s · 00:08:05**
Same corner of the screen, bolt gone. **34%**, a soft frame, **29%.** Faster now.

**06 · PLATE+UI · 1.21s · 00:09:15**
Macro, her phone flat on the walnut beside the laptop. A stopwatch is running: **00:15:12.**
Twelve points of battery in fifteen minutes. The film never explains this and doesn't need to.

## ACT II — SHE ASKS, NOAH MEASURES
**00:10:20 – 00:16:20 (frames 260–404, 6.00s)**

**07 · LIVE · 1.42s · 00:10:20**
Macro across the keyboard, low angle. Her fingers type — deliberate, not frantic. Keys catch the
lamp. No readable text anywhere in frame.

**08 · PLATE+UI · 1.58s · 00:12:06**
The Noah window, dark "Lantern" theme. Her sentence completes in the composer:
**"My battery keeps dying way too fast."** The caret blinks once. Send.

**09 · LIVE · 1.00s · 00:13:20**
Macro, the exhaust vent on the laptop's side. Air moving hard, a faint heat shimmer, dust motes

**10 · PLATE+UI · 1.21s · 00:14:20**
Noah's checks tick through by name — battery health, energy impact sampled over 60 seconds,
login items, idle power state. Named checks, not a progress bar.

**11 · LIVE · 0.79s · 00:16:01**
Macro, the heel of her hand resting on the aluminium beside the trackpad. It is warm. She lifts
it very slightly. One second, no dialogue, and the audience understands the symptom in the body.

## ACT III — THE CAUSE, AND THE GATE
**00:16:20 – 00:23:01 (frames 404–553, 6.21s)**

The centre of the film. Everything before this is setup; everything after is proof.

**12 · PLATE+UI · 2.21s · 00:16:20**
Noah names it. `NorthwindSyncHelper` — 94.2 average energy impact, 61% of a core, for four
hours — *stuck retrying a failed upload, so the Mac never drops into low-power idle.* One
sentence a non-technical person can repeat to someone else.

**13 · PLATE+UI · 2.21s · 00:19:01**
**What Noah will do.** Two numbered lines, the exact process and the exact file, the reassurance
that Northwind Drive still works, and the backup. Two buttons: **APPROVE** carrying the Aurora
gradient, **Not now** as a quiet ghost. Nothing is happening. Noah is waiting. *Hold this frame.
It is the ad.*

**14 · LIVE · 1.00s · 00:21:06**
Macro, her hand arrives at the trackpad. Fingertip settles. A beat of deliberate stillness — the
decision is hers and the film gives her the time to make it.

**15 · LIVE · 0.79s · 00:22:06**
Tight close-up, the fingertip presses. The trackpad's haptic thunk. The only percussive human
action in the spot.

## ACT IV — DONE, AND CHECKED AGAIN
**00:23:01 – 00:28:11 (frames 553–683, 5.42s)**

**16 · PLATE+UI · 1.42s · 00:23:01**
Two lines confirm in teal — the process quit, the login item disabled and backed up. **Done.**
**1.8s.** Beneath it: *Undo this change.* No theatre, no dramatic bar filling.

**17 · LIVE · 0.79s · 00:24:11**
The same vent, the same angle as clip 09. The air slows. The shimmer stops. This is the payoff
of a shot most people won't consciously remember, which is exactly why it works.

**18 · PLATE+UI · 2.21s · 00:25:06**
**Same check, run again.** Before and after, side by side: background energy impact 94.2 → 0.4,
idle CPU 61% of a core → 2%, estimated battery life 2h 06m → 5h 41m. Footnoted honestly:
*Measured on this Mac, tonight. Yours will differ.*

**19 · LIVE · 1.00s · 00:27:11**
Medium. Her shoulders drop about an inch and she lets out a breath. **Relief, not rescue.** No
smile to camera, no triumph. She just stops bracing.

## Panel-level notes for the boards artist

- **Eyeline is constant.** She looks at the screen, or at her hand, or down. She never looks at
  camera. Not once, including the end.
- **The camera never whips, never zooms, never handhelds hard.** Slow dolly, slow slider, one
  rack focus per shot at most. Micro-handheld float on the live-action shots only, ±2px.
- **Three shots are deliberately still**: 13 (the approval), 18 (the proof), 21 (the end card).
  In a spot this cut-up, stillness is what makes those three land.
- **The room does not change.** Same lamp position, same curtain, same mug, same cable coil, in
  clip 01 and clip 20. Anything that moves between them will be read as a continuity error.
- **Clips 09 and 17 are the same setup.** Shoot/generate them as one pair from one reference
  frame so the payoff registers as the same vent, not a similar one.
