# Noah — "Mine has one app on it"

**Format:** vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) · **Runtime:** 82s · **Platform:** YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels
**Reference shape:** the "Mac apps worth paying for" listicle — restructured so Noah is the number 1 and only pick.

**Why this structure works:** the reference video's #1 app is a cleaner. That is the exact thing Noah's
positioning exists to answer ("CleanMyMac cleans. Noah fixes what's actually wrong."). So the video keeps
the familiar listicle rhythm, then collapses the list to one — and earns that with the approval moment.

**Voice check (from BRAND.md):** Noah is always third person. First person belongs to the narrator.
Short declaratives. No hype adjectives. The word "scan" appears only as the thing the *cleaner* does.
Nothing here says "free", "trial", "subscription", or "IT".

## The named-competitor variant (beat 2)

Beat 2 says "a cleaner" rather than naming one. The brand's own positioning line names CleanMyMac
directly. If you want the sharper cut, swap beat 2's first line to:

> "Here's what usually happens. Your Mac gets slow, so you install CleanMyMac."

Sharper, more shareable, and legally a comparative claim — worth a second opinion before publishing.
The generic version lands nearly as hard and carries none of that.
