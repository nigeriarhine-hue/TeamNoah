## Through-line
**The issue-card stack** (`prop_card_stack`)

A pile of identical grey cards, each stamped with a vague phrase, grows beside the laptop as the fake problem count climbs, peaks at the approval moment, then unwinds.

_Resolution:_ In the payoff the stack collapses flat and leaves exactly one card face up, with the actual cause named on it in plain type.

## Brand guardrails applied
- Noah is always third person — never "I will fix it".
- Order is symptom -> real cause -> approval, per the brand kit's core messaging rule.
- Banned words avoided throughout: "IT", "scan" as the value, "no subscription", "free to use", "trial".
- No ark, no boat, no flood, no cape, no cyber/neon/circuit-board imagery.
- Aurora gradient appears once only — on the approve button in block 9 — never as wallpaper.
- Teal = confirmation, amber = caution. Used for nothing decorative.
- Real Macs, real desks, real rooms, daylight. A calm person, shoulders down.
