**Length:** ~40s · **Format:** 16:9 · **Tone:** natural, dry, no sell until the end
**Cast:** TOM — white man, 34. ELLIE — white woman, 32. Married, easy with each other.
**Location:** spare-room home office, real house, blue dusk outside, one warm lamp on.

The real complaint this is built from (verbatim, from the user):

> "Well, I think that this 2015 imac is booting into Sequoia via openpatch now when started

## Why the screens say what they say

The fabricated screens are built from the real app's own layout and wording
(`IMG_0583`–`IMG_0595`), re-pointed at the iMac complaint. Every number is consistent
across the plan and the result — nothing appears in the result that the plan didn't set up:

- 9 login items → trim 6 → **9 → 3**
- startup disk **23 GB free** + **18 GB cleared** = **41 GB free**
- Spotlight **~25 min left** in the plan, **~25 min** in the result

Step 4 of the plan is deliberately a refusal — Noah leaves the OpenCore root patches alone
and says why. It's the beat the whole spot turns on, and it's what Ellie's "because it
asked first" pays off.

The result card also declines to oversell: *"It won't feel like a new Mac — it's 2015
hardware running a 2026 OS — but this is the part you can get back."*
